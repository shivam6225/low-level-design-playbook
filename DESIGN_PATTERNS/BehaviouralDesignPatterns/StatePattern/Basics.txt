Suppose you are making Direction Service
--> Implement ETA
--> Implement Left/Right etc.. route directions

Direction Service will depend on
--Walking , Cycling , Car , Train
These are nothing but State - Transport
They heavily depend on State

Problems:
Messy if-else chains
Tight Dependencies
More Transport Mode means
---> difficult to read and update
---> breaks the open/closed principle

Direction Service -> Context (Which keeps track)

State -> Interface that defines the method all states should have

Concrete State -> Actual State Classes from Interface


Examples:
Music Player
--Play , Pause , Stop
Order Tracking
ATM
--Idle,card inserted , pin verify
Traffic Light System
-- red , yellow , green
